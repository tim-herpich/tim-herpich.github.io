# Welcome to my Website!


## **About Me**

Since 2020 I have been a **consultant** in the financial services industry with foocus on European **insurance companies** and currently I am working at the Zurich office of a German consulting boutique. My expertise lies in the **mathematical modeling of (non-)market financial risks** and the **development of bespoke software solutions** tailored to client needs.

Notable accomplishments include leading the development and implementation of an OECD-compliant [pricing framework](https://www.serv-ch.com/en/about-us/news/detail/dealing-with-the-non-marketable/) for export credit risks and forecasting the implications of the [Review of the Solvency II Directive](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-5481-2024-INIT/en/pdf) on the risk capital of European insurers.

My academic background includes an **MBA** I obtained in 2024 from [Mannheim Business School](https://www.mannheim-business-school.com/). In 2020, I received a **PhD in Statistical Physics** from the University of Luxembourg in an internationally renowned research group under the supervision of [Prof. M. Esposito](https://sites.google.com/site/massimilianoespositogennaro/home). My doctoral research focused on **nonequilibrium thermodynamics**, phase transitions and **Monte-Carlo simulations**, culminating in multiple [peer-reviewed publications](https://tim-herpich.github.io/publications/) and various [talks](https://tim-herpich.github.io/talks/) at international conferences. Before my doctoral studies, I obtained a MSc with distinction in Theoretical Physics from Leipzig University in 2015 amd a BSc in Physics from the Chemnitz University of Technology in 2013. 

Moreoever, I am currently sitting for the Level III **CFA** exam with the goal of becoming a member of the [CFA Society Switzerland](https://cfasocietyswitzerland.org/).
