My CFA Journey: From Curiosity to Charterholder

30 October 2025·444 words·3 mins
Finance Personal Growth CFA Learning Career Finance Achievement
How It Began My journey with the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) Program began out of curiosity rather than ambition. I wanted to understand financial systems more deeply, and the CFA curriculum appeared to offer a structured path. At the time, I had little idea of the scope, discipline, and time that the program would require. What started as an interest in learning soon became a multi-year process of structured study and reflection.
