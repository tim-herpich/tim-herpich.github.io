  <h2>💼 Profile</h2>
  <p>

    <a href="https://www.d-fine.com/en/locations/switzerland/">European consulting boutique</a>. I consult at the intersection of technology, risk, and asset management for insurance and asset management clients in Switzerland and Germany.
