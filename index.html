    Welcome To My Website

    💼 Profile

    I am a manager in financial services with a strong quantitative and technical background, currently based in the Zurich office of a European consulting boutique. I consult at the intersection of technology, risk, and asset management for insurance and asset management clients in Switzerland and Germany. My work focuses on translating regulatory and risk requirements—such as Solvency II & UK, DORA, and OECD guidelines—into practical, data-driven solutions that strengthen risk frameworks and investment processes.

    I hold an MBA from Mannheim Business School and a PhD in Statistical Physics, obtained in an internationally renowned research group under the supervision of Prof. M. Esposito. My doctoral research focused on nonequilibrium thermodynamics and Monte Carlo simulations, resulting in multiple peer-reviewed publications and conference presentations. I also hold an MSc with distinction in Theoretical Physics.

    In addition, I am a CFA Charterholder and an active member of CFA Society Switzerland, complementing my quantitative background with expertise in investment analysis, portfolio management, and professional ethics.

    💡 Why This Website?

    This website serves as a platform to share insights from my work in financial services and academia. On my blog, I discuss topics related to risk and asset management, reflect on practical experiences, and engage with ongoing debates in the field.
