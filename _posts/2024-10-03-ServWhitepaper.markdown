## <i class="fas fa-balance-scale"></i> The Challenge: Pricing the Unpriceable  
**Export Credit Agencies (ECAs)** <i class="fas fa-shield-alt"></i> insure risks that commercial insurers avoid—long-term, high-risk, and often politically uncertain transactions. While pricing **export credit insurance policies** is already complex, pricing *modifications* to these policies is an even greater challenge. There are no regulatory guidelines, no established market benchmarks, and each case exhibits idiosyncratic risk profiles.

## <i class="fas fa-calculator"></i> A Unified Approach to Pricing Insurance Modifications  
Our whitepaper introduces a **mathematical framework** that brings structure to this problem. It starts by examining existing models for pricing export credit insurance and then introduces a central concept: the **Specific Risk Premium**. This quantity provides a way to standardize pricing across different export credit insurance modifications by quantifying idiosyncratic risks and aligning them with the corresponding protection offered by the ECA.  

The result? A **consistent pricing framework** that enables ECAs to apply a unified approach across a diverse range of export business cases—ensuring fairness, efficiency, and better risk management.

## <i class="fas fa-handshake"></i> Why This Matters  
In an industry where no two transactions are alike, a structured approach to pricing modifications is essential. Whether extending repayment terms, increasing coverage, or adjusting policy terms post-inception, the ability to price these changes fairly is crucial for ECAs, banks, and exporters.
