<h2><i class="fas fa-balance-scale"></i> The Challenge</h2>
<p><strong>Export Credit Agencies (ECAs)</strong> insure risks that commercial insurers avoid: long-term, high-risk, and often politically uncertain transactions. While pricing <strong>export credit insurance policies</strong> is already complex, pricing <em>modifications</em> to these policies is an even greater challenge. There are no regulatory guidelines, no established market benchmarks, and each export case exhibits unique characteristics and therefore idiosyncratic risk profiles.</p>

<h2><i class="fas fa-calculator"></i> A Unified Mathematical Framework</h2>
<p>Colleagues from <strong>Schweizerische Exportrisikoversicherung (SERV)</strong> and I tackled this challenge in a <strong>whitepaper</strong>:</p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://www.serv-ch.com/fileadmin/user_upload/files/Praemien/SERV-Pricing_Framework.pdf" target="_blank">
    <img src="/assets/images/serv_whitepaper_cover.png" alt="The Whitepaper" width="500">
  </a>
</p>

<p>Our whitepaper introduces a <strong>mathematical framework</strong> that brings structure to this problem. It starts by examining existing models for the initial pricing of export credit insurances and then introduces a central quantity: the <strong>Specific Risk Premium</strong>. As shown, this quantity allows for a standardized pricing across different export credit insurance modifications by aligning the idiosyncratic risks with the corresponding protection offered by the ECA. The result? A <strong>pricing methodology</strong> that enables ECAs to apply a unified approach across a diverse range of export business cases — ensuring fairness, efficiency, and better risk management.</p>

<h2><i class="fas fa-handshake"></i> Why This Matters</h2>
<p>In an industry where no two transactions are alike, a structured approach to pricing modifications is essential. Whether extending repayment terms, increasing coverage, or adjusting policy terms post-inception, the ability to price these changes fairly is crucial for ECAs, banks, and exporters.</p>

<p><strong>Curious?</strong> Dive into the full whitepaper <strong><a href="https://www.serv-ch.com/en/about-us/news/detail/dealing-with-the-non-marketable/">here</a></strong> and explore how this framework helps bring order to the challenge of pricing export credit insurance modifications.</p>
